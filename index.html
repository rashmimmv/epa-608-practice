    EPA 608 Practice Exam — This free 20-question quiz covers core topics on the EPA Section 608 refrigerant certification exam, including refrigerant types, recovery, recycling, reclaiming, leak repair requirements, and technician certification rules.

    The EPA 608 exam is required under Section 608 of the Clean Air Act for any technician who purchases or works with refrigerants. Certification types include Type I (small appliances), Type II (high-pressure systems), Type III (low-pressure systems), and Universal (all types).
